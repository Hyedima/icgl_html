Who qualifies

Eligibility for ICGL guarantee support.

ICGL does not lend directly to businesses. Guarantee support is accessed through approved Participating Financial Institutions. Both the borrowing business and the PFI must meet the relevant criteria.

MSME / borrower eligibility

Criteria for businesses seeking guaranteed loans.

  • Must be a Micro, Small or Medium Enterprise registered in Nigeria
  • Business must be operational and generating, or capable of generating, revenue
  • Must be seeking a loan through an ICGL-approved PFI
  • Business must not be involved in ICGL exclusion-list activities
  • Owner(s) must not be on a financial crime or credit blacklist
  • Must demonstrate a viable purpose for the loan

PFI eligibility

Criteria for financial institutions to become ICGL partners.

  • Must be licensed and regulated by the Central Bank of Nigeria
  • Must have an active MSME lending portfolio
  • Must meet ICGL financial health requirements
  • Must have satisfactory AML/CFT compliance frameworks
  • Must sign the ICGL Participation Agreement
  • Must meet reporting and information-sharing obligations
How it works

Four steps to a guarantee.

01

Approach a PFI

Express your interest in accessing a guaranteed loan.

02

PFI assessment

The PFI conducts its own credit analysis.

03

ICGL review

ICGL reviews the guarantee application.

04

Guarantee issued

Upon approval, the PFI disburses the loan.
