Our Services

What We Do

ICGL delivers core services designed to strengthen access to finance and support sustainable economic growth. Through its services, ICGL shares lending risk with financial institutions, encouraging them to extend credit to viable businesses that might otherwise face financing constraints. In addition, ICGL provides Technical Assistance to partner financial institutions and businesses, helping strengthen capacity and improve risk management practices.

Finance that moves business
01 / ICGL offering

Individual Guarantee

A partial credit guarantee issued against a single, specific loan facility extended by a PFI to an eligible MSME.

  • Designed for one borrower and one facility at a time.
  • ICGL performs direct appraisal of the proposed facility and borrower profile.
  • Best suited for larger or more bespoke loans where the risk needs targeted assessment.
02 / ICGL offering

Blanket Guarantee

Cover extended across a defined class of qualifying loans, allowing PFIs to scale lending to MSMEs without seeking approval on each transaction.

  • Covers a portfolio of eligible loans within a defined segment or product line.
  • Accelerates credit decisions and reduces processing bottlenecks for PFIs.
  • Supports repeat lending and expansion in sectors with strong developmental impact.
03 / ICGL offering

Portfolio Guarantee

Risk-sharing applied across a PFI’s wider MSME loan portfolio, diversifying exposure across many borrowers and sectors.

  • Applies to a broader pool of MSME loans rather than a single facility.
  • Improves portfolio diversification and helps PFIs manage concentration risk.
  • Encourages sustainable growth in SME lending and deeper real-sector penetration.
04 / ICGL offering

Technical Assistance

Specialised capacity building and advisory services to enhance PFI and MSME operational efficiency.
