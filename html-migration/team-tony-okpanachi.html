Dr. Tony Okpanachi

Non-Executive Director

Banking expertise in service of impact.

Dr. Tony Okpanachi is a seasoned banker with over 27 years of experience and Managing Director/CEO of the Development Bank of Nigeria.

He holds an MBA from Manchester Business School, a Master of Science in Economics from the University of Lagos, and a Bachelor of Science in Economics from Ahmadu Bello University.
