About us

Why we exist

Impact Credit Guarantee Limited (ICGL) bridges the financing gap faced by Nigerian MSMEs by reducing the risks that limit access to credit. As a wholly owned subsidiary of the Development Bank of Nigeria (DBN), ICGL partners with financial institutions to share lending risk through tailored credit guarantee solutions.

Individual Guarantee

For single loans to a single, known borrower, ICGL performs direct appraisal.

Blanket Guarantee

Authority to guarantee a portfolio of loans is vested in the PFI.

Portfolio Guarantee

Guarantees a portfolio of loans to a target segment, up to a defined ceiling.

Technical Assistance

Specialised capacity building and advisory services to enhance PFI and MSME operational efficiency.

Participating Financial Institutions

To drive inclusive economic growth, we partner with a robust network of accredited lenders across Nigeria.
