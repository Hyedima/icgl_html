FAQs

General
Impact Credit Guarantee Limited shares risk with participating financial institutions so they can lend more confidently to Nigerian MSMEs and small corporates.
No. Businesses access guarantee support through approved Participating Financial Institutions.
A guarantee gives the lending institution additional risk comfort, which can improve access to credit for eligible businesses.
Eligibility and process
Eligible MSMEs and small corporates operating in Nigeria can apply through an approved PFI.
Timing depends on the PFI credit assessment and the completeness of the submitted information.
